SAI3D: Segment Any Instance in 3D Scenes

Yingda Yin, Yuzheng Liu, Baoquan Chen; Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2024, pp. 3292-3302
Advancements in 3D instance segmentation have traditionally been tethered to the availability of annotated datasets. We introduce SAI3D, a novel zero-shot 3D instance segmentation approach.
